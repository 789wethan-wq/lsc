5. What this does and doesn't establish
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Does establish: a fresh clone of this exact commit, following only
the documented `make venv`-equivalent setup, reproduces the test suite
and the two most recently added benchmark scripts' numbers exactly --
not approximately, not "close enough given MC noise," bit-for-bit.
That closes the specific gap this reproducibility push was about: a
script whose output is cited in the paper is actually runnable from
scratch, not just present in the repository.

Does not establish: correctness of the underlying statistics or DGP
assumptions (that's what the paper's own theory sections and tests/
argue for), or reproducibility of the FRED/ALFRED real-data sections
(covered separately by `make realdata`/`make realtime` against the
pinned data/ snapshots, not re-verified in this pass).

This log itself was generated by running the commands above against a
real clone and pasting their actual output -- not reconstructed from
memory or from the numbers already reported earlier in this project's
history.

--- Superseding note, added 2026-07-23 ---
The exp14_mixed_channel.py numbers reproduced above (detect
raw=0.553/0.410/0.213, arima=0.517/0.560/0.623, combined=0.493/0.490/
0.457 at SNR 0.1/0.5/2.0) are from BEFORE a calibration-seed bug in

used seed0=200_000 (this repo's evaluation seed block) instead of
100_000, producing a threshold 9-19% too low. This log's bit-for-bit
match to a since-superseded commit is still an accurate historical
record of that commit's behavior -- reproducibility of a buggy result
is still reproducibility -- but the numbers above no longer match
either the current script or the current PAPER_DRAFT.md, which cites
the corrected values (raw=0.550/0.390/0.183, arima=0.480/0.537/0.560,
combined=0.500/0.500/0.470). See the CHANGELOG entry for the fresh
threshold-comparison verification of the fix.
